Our Story

Black Busted is a proposed South African streetwear brand built around youth culture, creativity and self-expression.

The brand message focuses on starting from limited circumstances while continuing to work towards creating something meaningful.

What Black Busted Represents

  • Creativity
  • Individuality
  • Self-expression
  • Ambition
  • Youth culture

Our Brand Message

"Black and broke but Tryna make a Difference."

This message represents the idea that people can work towards their goals regardless of where they start.
